# Checklist — Discovery Questions (Deliverable #6)

> Use this **after** generating your Discovery Questions and **before** gate day.

## Completeness check

### Question count and prioritisation

- [ ] At least **8–10 questions** drafted (you'll use ~5–7 in the live round; having extras lets you adapt)
- [ ] Questions ranked by **design impact** — highest-impact first
- [ ] Top 5 questions are the ones you'd ask if the stakeholder cuts the session short

### Question structure (each question)

- [ ] The question itself is specific and concise (≤ 2 sentences)
- [ ] **Tension/gap targeted** is named — references a specific item from your analysis (assumption log entry, system gap, delegation boundary, breakpoint)
- [ ] **Design decision at stake** is explicit — "If the answer is X, I would [design choice A]; if Y, I would [design choice B]"
- [ ] If removing the "design decision" line leaves nothing meaningful, the question is generic — rewrite or cut

### Question quality

- [ ] No **generic openers** ("Walk me through your process", "Tell me about your team", "What are your pain points")
- [ ] At least 2 questions target **lived work vs documented process** gaps from your Cognitive Load Map
- [ ] At least 1 question targets a **system/data gap** from Deliverable #5
- [ ] At least 1 question targets a **delegation boundary** — where you're unsure if a task should be agentic vs human-led
- [ ] At least 1 question tests a **specific assumption** from the Agent Purpose Document's assumption log

### Live round readiness

- [ ] Questions are framed so a **busy, sceptical stakeholder** would engage (not academic or theoretical)
- [ ] Follow-up probes prepared for likely evasion (e.g. "It depends" → "Can you give me two cases where it went differently?")
- [ ] Questions are independent enough that you can skip or reorder if the conversation goes a different direction

## Anti-pattern check

| Anti-pattern | How to detect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Generic discovery questions** | "What tools do you use?" / "How does your team work?" | Tie to a specific tension: "The tracker shows you update it before Workday — is that always true, or only when…?" |
| **Questions with no design impact** | Interesting to know but wouldn't change the agent design | Apply the "If X vs Y" test — if both answers lead to the same design, cut it |
| **All questions about systems** | No questions about judgment, exceptions, or delegation boundaries | Add questions probing cognitive hotspots and lived-work patterns |
| **Leading questions** | "Don't you think AI could help with X?" | Reframe as neutral: "When X happens, what do you do today?" |
| **Too many questions** | 15+ questions with no prioritisation | Rank by design impact; flag top 5 as "must-ask" |

## Feed-forward

- Discovery question answers (from the live round) update the **Assumption Log** across all deliverables
- High-confidence answers may shift delegation archetype assignments in Deliverable #2
- This deliverable demonstrates **FDE judgment** — it's the most direct signal when domain knowledge is shallow
