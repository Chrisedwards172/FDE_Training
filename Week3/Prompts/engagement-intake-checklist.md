# Checklist — Engagement Intake & Scope (D#2)

> Use **after** generating your Engagement Intake to verify completeness.

## Completeness Check

### Business Context

- [ ] Company overview (size, market, geography, business model)
- [ ] Current operational model (how coordinators work today)
- [ ] Recent history relevant to engagement (prior AI failures, growth trajectory)

### Stakeholder Map

- [ ] At least 4 stakeholders identified (CEO, coordinators, hospital admins, nurses)
- [ ] Each has: role, authority level, attitude toward the project, key concern
- [ ] At least one **conflicting incentive** pair identified (e.g. CEO wants speed vs coordinators fear replacement)
- [ ] Decision-maker vs influencer vs blocker distinguished

### Constraints

- [ ] Timeline constraint stated (CEO's 8 weeks) with honest assessment of feasibility
- [ ] Technical constraints (existing systems, integration points, data quality)
- [ ] Regulatory constraints (healthcare staffing compliance, credentialing, privacy)
- [ ] Team/budget constraints if discoverable

### Risk Register

- [ ] At least 5 risks identified
- [ ] Each has: description, likelihood (H/M/L), impact (H/M/L), mitigation
- [ ] **Adoption risk** explicitly named (two prior AI project failures)
- [ ] **Scope creep risk** named (CEO's ambition vs realistic delivery)

### MVP Scope

- [ ] Specific workflows named (not "improve staffing" — which part of staffing?)
- [ ] User groups in MVP identified
- [ ] Geographic or functional scope bounded
- [ ] Phase 1 vs Phase 2 boundary clear

### Out of Scope

- [ ] At least 3 explicit exclusions with reasoning
- [ ] Exclusions reference the CEO's requests that were deprioritised
- [ ] Each exclusion has a "when it comes back" note (Phase 2, post-validation, etc.)

## Anti-Pattern Self-Check

| Anti-pattern | Signal | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Everything in scope** | MVP includes every workflow the CEO mentioned | Cut to what's deliverable in 8 weeks; name what moves |
| **Stakeholder map is a list** | Names without incentives, attitudes, or conflict | Add what each person wants and where they clash |
| **Risk-free risk register** | All risks are "Low likelihood, Low impact" | Be honest — adoption risk is real, timeline is aggressive |
| **Scope boundary is verbal** | "We agreed to focus on X" without documenting what's out | Write the out-of-scope section as if it's a contract clause |

## Feed-Forward

Scope definition constrains D#3 (architecture only covers what's in scope) and D#6 (client feedback response defends the scope boundary you set here).
