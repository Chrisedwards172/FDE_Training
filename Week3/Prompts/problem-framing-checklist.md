# Checklist — Problem Framing & Success Metrics (D#1)

> Use **after** generating your Problem Framing to verify completeness.

## Completeness Check

### Problem Statement

- [ ] States what is **actually broken** — not the CEO's aspiration ("10x without 10x-ing")
- [ ] Grounded in discovery evidence (what you heard in the Thursday session)
- [ ] Identifies root causes, not symptoms
- [ ] Distinguishes between problems the agentic system can solve vs organisational/process problems

### Stakeholder Success Criteria

- [ ] **MedFlex (business)** success criteria defined — revenue, margin, coordinator capacity
- [ ] **Hospitals (customers)** success criteria defined — fill rate, quality, response time
- [ ] **Nurses (workers)** success criteria defined — match quality, experience, retention
- [ ] Each stakeholder perspective has at least one metric the others don't share

### Measurable KPIs

- [ ] Every KPI has: baseline (current) → target → measurement method
- [ ] At least one KPI per stakeholder perspective
- [ ] No vague metrics ("improve efficiency") — all are specific and measurable
- [ ] Time horizon stated (when is "success" measured — 8 weeks? 6 months?)

### Scope Exclusions

- [ ] At least one thing the CEO implied is in scope that you've explicitly excluded with reasoning
- [ ] Exclusions are framed as "not in MVP" rather than "not important"

## Anti-Pattern Self-Check

| Anti-pattern | Signal | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Restating the brief** | Your framing reads like the scenario summary | Rewrite: what did you learn in discovery that the brief didn't tell you? |
| **Unmeasurable success** | KPIs like "better matching" or "faster placement" | Add numbers: "fill rate from X% to Y%, measured weekly" |
| **Single-stakeholder framing** | Only MedFlex metrics; nurses and hospitals ignored | Add a row per stakeholder — their incentives diverge |
| **CEO appeasement** | Framing accepts "8 weeks" without questioning scope | State what's achievable in 8 weeks and what needs longer |

## Feed-Forward

Problem framing directly shapes D#2 (what's in/out of scope) and D#3 (which workflows become agentic). If the framing is vague, scope and architecture will be vague.
